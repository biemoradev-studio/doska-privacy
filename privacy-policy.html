Privacy Policy

Doska: Visual Notes · Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Developer: Biemora Apps

This Privacy Policy explains how Doska: Visual Notes (the “App”), developed by Biemora Apps (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App on Android.

1. Information you create in the App

The App lets you create visual boards, notes, drawings, and presentations. The App is designed to work with content stored on your device. We do not operate a user-account service or a cloud synchronization service for this content in the current version.

Content that you choose to export, share, or open using your device's file picker is handled according to the action you request. If you share a file with another app or service, that recipient's privacy practices may apply.

2. Advertising and information processed by Google

The App integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads technology for advertising. When the advertising SDK is used, Google may automatically collect or receive information including:

  • IP address, which may be used to infer approximate location;
  • device and advertising identifiers, subject to device settings and applicable restrictions;
  • app interactions, such as app launches and ad views or interactions; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

Google may process this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and support advertising-related analytics, in accordance with its policies. Depending on your region and choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Availability of privacy choices depends on the App's implemented consent controls and applicable law.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services.

3. In-app purchases

The App may offer an optional purchase to remove advertisements, processed by Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment processing and may process purchase and transaction information under its own privacy policy. The App may receive purchase status and related transaction identifiers needed to unlock or restore purchased features and help prevent misuse. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Purchasing ad removal does not necessarily remove or delete information that Google processed before the purchase.

4. Why information is processed

Information is processed, as applicable, to provide App functionality, save user-created content on the device, deliver and measure advertising, verify in-app purchases, maintain security, prevent fraud, and diagnose technical problems.

5. Sharing and service providers

We use Google services for advertising and in-app purchasing. Information processed through those services may be shared with or received by Google as described above. We do not sell the notes, boards, or drawings that you create in the App. Information you intentionally export or share is disclosed according to your chosen action.

6. Data security, storage, and retention

Content saved locally remains on your device unless you export, share, or delete it. You can generally remove locally stored App data using the App's deletion features or Android's app storage controls. Uninstalling the App may remove its locally stored data, subject to device backups and files exported outside the App.

Google may retain advertising, diagnostic, and purchase-related information under its own applicable retention rules. Google Mobile Ads reports using encrypted connections for transmitted SDK data. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your privacy choices

  • You may adjust Android advertising privacy settings, including resetting or deleting an advertising ID where available.
  • You can decide whether to export or share your notes and boards.
  • You may delete your locally saved content or clear App storage through your device settings.
  • Where required and available, you may manage advertising consent through the App's privacy options.
  • You may contact us about privacy-related questions using the email below.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not specifically directed to children. We do not intentionally request children's personal details through an in-app registration system. If you believe information relating to a child has been improperly processed, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, service providers, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise its effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Biemora Apps at biemora.dev@gmail.com.

Note: This policy describes the App's intended current features and the third-party services identified above. It should be reviewed whenever the App's actual SDK configuration, data practices, or features change.
